Game Development Marketing & Design

Makerspace

Marketing collateral for Auckland Libraries' Makerspace program — promoting creative technology such as Game Dev, Music Production, Robotics and 3D Printers for local communities.

Behind the Design

The theme across all marketing collateral was reminiscent of 80s media and the surging popularity of arcade games. The technology theme of the Makerspace called for a bright neon "cyber" colour palette. The palette features green (renewal), turquoise/blue (positive emotions and empathy), purple (imagination), and pink (love and playfulness). These analogous colours create a feeling of creativity and fun in a digital environment, whilst the neon tones provide high visibility and nostalgia referencing early 80s arcade machines.

With the poster and Instagram post, I also created an arcade style game called "Library Mania". Key design considerations: easy to pick up, specific aspect ratios, and fun to generate interest in Makerspace.
