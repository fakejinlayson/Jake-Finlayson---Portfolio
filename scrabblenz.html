About

Contracted as the NZ Scrabble Livestream Producer, showcasing top games (Masters, Nationals, etc.) on YouTube. The role involves a multi-cam setup whilst streaming in commentators from across the globe to OBS and going live.

Additionally, I had further contracts with Scrabble NZ making a YouTube documentary-style video promoting Scrabble in New Zealand. This project involved using GeoLayers in Adobe After Effects, Premiere Pro for editing, iZotope RX11 to master audio, and Photoshop for any graphics.
