About the Project

The idea for this game was to create a 'Game for Impact'. Taking a unique spin on the common email scammer trope, we wanted to explore why people actually scam in the first place. By placing the player in the role of a scammer, we could explore the psychology behind it — narratively showcasing the desperation and moral dilemmas that lead people to scam others.

To best illustrate this morally-grey concept, inspiration was taken from Papers, Please, with a '90s nostalgic computer aesthetic wrapping the experience. Whilst the game is comedic, it was important that the world building and lore I created all linked back to real world issues.

Key Focuses

My key focuses for this project were programming the custom Technical Narrative Design system, Research & Narrative Design, the dynamic scoring system, optimisation and testing, and general Game Design.
