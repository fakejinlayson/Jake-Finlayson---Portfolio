The Everything Bagel and Debatle: two daily browser games I designed and built from scratch.

About

Daily games live or die on one idea that's quick to learn, different every day and fun to share. I wanted to make a small suite of them with a shared feel: a chunky pixel-art look, a numbered puzzle every day at midnight.

I handled everything end to end: the game design, the pixel-art interface, the front and back end, the content pipeline that produces each day's puzzle and the tools I use to check it before it goes live. Both games launched in 2026.

The Everything Bagel

A daily word puzzle shaped like a bagel. Four categories sit around the ring, and each segment needs an answer that fits the two categories either side of it. The centre needs one answer that fits all four. Rarer answers score more.

Play The Everything Bagel →
Debatle

A daily debate game. Every day there's one ridiculous motion, like whether the middle seat should get both armrests. You pick a side and argue it against an AI opponent over three short rounds: an opening, a rebuttal and a closing.
