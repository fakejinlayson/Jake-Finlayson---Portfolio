Music Production

tides
under all

An original album blending Botanica genres with Hyperpop elements, stylistically rooted in Videogame OST aesthetics.

About

Originally began as part of the OST Composing Jam of 2024, which I ended up scoring 39th out of 650 contestants. I further refined the concept and published it online to major DSPs such as Spotify with a new album cover I illustrated.

The album blends Botanica genres with Hyperpop elements whilst remaining stylistically a Videogame OST. Through the tracks, the genre explores someone's full life — from birth to death and what's after. Using symbolism of water, the album shows that the tides are a part of us all.

To achieve this sound, I used FL Studio, combining organic instruments, granular synthesis and digital synthesis.
