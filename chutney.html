CHUTNEY:
Space Survivor

A third person horde-shooting deck builder where you play as Chutney, shooting down hordes of enemies with his Omni-gun. Alter your attacks by collecting different cartridges and create unique synergies.

Trailer
Game Design

Player agency, choice and synergistic design are integral to roguelike games. Stats, weapons, enemies and the map all needed to allow players to make meaningful choices that impact their run. Using Vampire Survivors as inspiration, I helped design and implement a variety of weapons and level-ups that let players experiment with different playstyles.

I designed and implemented various enemy types, each with unique behaviours and attack patterns. It was crucial to ensure each enemy was correctly balanced to provide a fair challenge regardless of the weapon order used. I achieved this by maintaining a detailed spreadsheet tracking enemy stats, player damage outputs, and difficulty scaling across the game's progression.

The map is randomly generated each run, with different enemy spawns, allowing players to choose their own path — inspired by Slay the Spire's map.

Programming

I primarily used C# in Unity to program the game's systems, including AI behaviour, UI systems, random map generation, weapon mechanics, and the meta-progression system. I utilised Unity's built-in profiler to optimise performance.

I also programmed a custom audio solution to handle the game's dynamic radio system, as well as managing the multitude of sound effects occurring at any given time.

Music Production

The OST for CHUTNEY: Space Survivor incorporates a variety of genres including electroswing, disco, jazz and more. These take form as radio stations in the game, which are accessible through achievement unlocks.
