About

The channel is a personal project where I explore various aspects of game design, development, and the psychological principles that make games engaging and enjoyable. To create these videos I use a combination of gameplay footage mixed with motion graphics created in Adobe Illustrator and After Effects. For the audio I record using Adobe Audition, mix and master using iZotope RX11, then collate all clips in Premiere Pro.
