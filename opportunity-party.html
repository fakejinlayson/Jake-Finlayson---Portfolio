Volunteer photography, videography, video editing and marketing for The Opportunity Party's 2026 election campaign, the campaign that took them into Parliament.

About

I volunteered on The Opportunity Party's marketing team for the 2026 general election, shooting photos and video at events and editing short-form clips for their social channels. The work was fast-turnaround: film on the day, cut it down and get it posted while it was still news.

The clips below cover the range: a community event filmed and cut with animated captions and B-roll, an on-site visit to a farm running solar and battery storage, a conference speech, and a national TV interview reworked into a vertical reel for social.

The campaign made them the first "true" new party in New Zealand's MMP history to make it into Parliament: on the party vote alone, without a sitting MP or an electorate seat to carry them over the line.

The result
5.96% Party vote, over the 5% threshold
144,298 Party votes
7 MPs into Parliament
1st New party into Parliament on the party vote alone under MMP

Preliminary results, 2026 general election. Final results with special votes due 27 November 2026.
