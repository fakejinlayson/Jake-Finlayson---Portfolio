RIDDEN

A 2D side-scrolling space shooter controlled entirely by the mouse. Play as an armed maintenance drone aboard a spaceship overrun by aliens — collect organic matter, upgrade your systems, and fight through alien bosses.

Carousel
Game Design

The initial inspiration for RIDDEN was Dead Space — particularly how they utilised diegetic UI throughout the game, immersing the player further into the world. I wanted to make the game even more minimal, inspired by Downwell: a minimalist platformer with a simple colour palette where every input you can make has three or more different actions.

What interested me about both games was stripping back to core inputs, aesthetics and mechanics, whilst still creating breadth of interesting, immersive gameplay. The entire game's controls are with the mouse: right click to accelerate, left click to shoot (which also pushes you back). The colour palette is black and blue, with art style inspiration from Badland. All UI is diegetic like Dead Space. Lastly, the music adapts to the amount and type of enemies on screen, giving the player a sense of warning without UI indicators.

Programming

The engine chosen for the game was Unity. My responsibilities included the movement system, random map generation, the levelling system, and a custom technical audio solution which adapts depending on the type of enemies remaining in the room.
